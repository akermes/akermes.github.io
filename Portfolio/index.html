Who am I?

I started in the classroom teaching high school English. I stayed in learning because I like solving the problems behind it. Since then I have managed a recruitment team, run tools and automation for a support organization, designed graduate courses for three universities, and built an AI learning platform on my own.

Along the way I kept finding the same thing: most training problems are design problems. That is the work I do now.
