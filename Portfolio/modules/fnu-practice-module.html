    What to know before you start

    Anyone can take any step

    Any delivery team member may complete any step, apart from a few Courseware team steps. Steps 2 to 4 should go to three different people so nobody reviews their own work.

    Peer review replaces senior review

    A teammate reviews the batch and releases it to COMP. Your grade span Senior ID is there for questions the team cannot resolve.

    CAD populates only part of the screenmap

    Activity parts, section titles, and standards, plus the yellow rows in the CW Comp sheet. Everything else is yours to populate.

    Your documents must agree

    Any markup decision you make in one place carries into the others: the SE, the TE, the screenmap, and later the CAT.

    Say "Flexible Novel Unit." "FNU" is for internal use only. Anything a teacher or student sees uses the full name.

    Screen label formula

    [Part letter][2-digit screen number][S or ST]_[Authoring tool]_[Template]_[Page type]

    • Part letter only for multi-part activities. Number screens in order.
    • S is student-facing only. ST also has teacher-facing content (teach notes or annos).
    • Only Ceres screens and polls need a template. Only Ceres screens need a page type.
    • Non-Ceres screens are always S.
    • Resurfaced screens do not get a new number: Resurface [Activity # Screen #].
    Authoring toolUse for
    CeresActivity content: instruction, exercises
    ReaderAny reading passage (screen number only, no template or page type)
    Graphic OrganizerA prebuilt PDF asset (comment and screenmap row, no template link)
    Scoring GuideScoring an Embedded Assessment (comment and screenmap row, no screen number)
    Writing ExperienceOne asset per unit, placed in Activity 1 (screenmap row only)
    PollCheckpoints
    MarkupCeres page typeUse for
    IntroIntroductionPreview and Learning Targets screens only
    InstInstructionModels a concept
    ExExerciseRequires student participation
    CloClosingConcludes a lesson

    COMP replies

    Reply to your label comment and begin each reply with "COMP:".

    • ST screens: all teach notes and annos for that screen.
    • Images: spec ID, alt text of 150 characters or fewer, and the image modal header ("Image" unless there is a caption). Complex images also need a long description.
    • Grades 9 to 12 Preview and Learning Targets: the Essential Questions.
    • Callout boxes: the H2 and which content goes in the box.
    • Tables: "Please recreate the table without student annos" and "Please prepopulate the OTR widget with the following content: [labels or headings]".
    • Resurfaced responses: "Resurface student responses from Activity X.X Screen XX." Both screens need an OTR widget.
    • Complex templates: templates with an evaluator, such as multiple choice, text select, text entry, and dropdown, need a Ceres table pasted into the manuscript.

    Step 2 checklist

      Peer review watch list

      • Incorrect template for the exercise
      • More or different information needed for the COMP vendor
      • Inconsistent screen numbering (number, S or ST, authoring tool, template, or page type) across the manuscripts, screenmap, and CAT
      • Incorrect or missing information in the CAT or the spec log
      • Course shells set up incorrectly in Ceres
      • Incorrect CAT row statuses or a missing filter
      • Missing asset rows

      Do not flag matters of preference. If you would have chunked it differently but the current choice also works, leave it.
