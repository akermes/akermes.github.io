Consultative Sales Training and Evaluation

A coaching program with measured results: shorter calls, more calls, more enrollments.

Enrollment advisors were spending too long on calls that went nowhere. Leads sat in the pipeline, and conversion rates were flat.

As the team's manager, I ran a consultative sales training program in Mindtickle and built the evaluation around it: scored role-plays, QA-scored call recordings against a rubric, and dashboards in Tableau that tied the two together.

The evaluation results drove focused call coaching. Call length dropped 17%, call volume rose 12%, stale leads left the pipeline sooner, and team enrollments increased 15%. I reported KPIs, growth areas, and an improvement plan with targets to leadership.

The design process

Initial design

I wrote an evaluation plan before the training ran: what we would measure, how, and what good looked like. That meant the training had a target, not just a completion date.

Storyboarding

The rubric was the storyboard. It defined the behaviors we wanted on calls, which shaped both the Mindtickle content and the QA scoring.

Development

Training ran in Mindtickle. Role-plays and recorded calls were scored, the scores fed the Tableau dashboard, and the dashboard drove weekly coaching. The cycle repeated until the numbers moved.
