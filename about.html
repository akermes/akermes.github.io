About me

Teacher, manager, builder. Instructional designer by choice.

I've spent 13 years in learning, on both sides of the desk.

I started as a high school English teacher. I led a professional learning community, chaired my department, and ran professional development for other teachers. I learned to use assessment data to decide what to teach next, and I learned that good materials make good teaching easier.

At Pearson I managed a recruitment team of about 22 people. I was the hiring manager, ran onboarding and coaching, and built the Tableau and Power BI dashboards we used to track performance. When I finished my M.Ed. in Learning Design and Technologies at Arizona State, I moved into instructional design and built 30+ graduate modules in Canvas for nursing and cybersecurity programs. Later I managed tools and automation for a support organization, where I served as the unofficial product owner and ran a Microsoft Copilot adoption pilot that cut handling time by 42%.

Today I design Grade 9 literacy content for McGraw Hill's Summit! and Soar! program and run The Learning Loop, where I take on contract design work for companies and universities. On the side I built Pathways, an AI-powered homeschool platform on the Claude API. I work in ADDIE, SAM, and Agile, and I measure what I build.

I live in the White Mountains of Arizona and work remotely.
