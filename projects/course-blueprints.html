Work / Course Blueprints

Course Blueprints

Course-level design documents that let a whole team build in the same direction.

Before a course is built, someone has to decide what it covers, what it leaves out, how it is assessed, and what has to change from the version that already exists. That document is the blueprint, and it is where most course problems are either prevented or created.

Through Lumina Datamatics, I have written blueprints for two very different clients. For Western Governors University, I co-authored the course design for Cloud Strategy, a three-unit graduate course in the MSIT program, defining scope, out-of-scope boundaries, and the strategic (not technical) framing of every topic. For Cengage's ed2go, I am the Learning Designer on the Canadian localization of a 26-lesson Physiotherapy Assistant course: course description, outcomes, objectives, global edits, and a page-level revision tracker for every lesson.

Both blueprints run through a structured review with a subject matter expert and an education specialist. My job is to make the first call on every item, document the reasoning, and leave a trail the client can approve line by line.

The design process

Course analysis

Each blueprint starts with a course analysis: what exists, what the client's standards require, and what the gap is. For the localization, that meant reading all 26 lessons against Canadian scope of practice, terminology, and regulatory references.

The blueprint as storyboard

The template is the storyboard: course metadata, build instructions, in and out of scope, outcomes and objectives, then lesson-by-lesson revision rows with a category and a rationale.

Review

Review runs in a fixed order (learning designer, subject matter expert, education specialist), and every row carries approvals from each. Nothing moves to build until the trail is complete.

The finished project

Excerpts from the two blueprints, with team names removed.

Cloud Strategy (WGU, MSIT): scope definition

Three competency units. Project-assessed. The course is strictly strategic: learners are not expected to set up cloud solutions, but must understand cloud infrastructure well enough to recommend a business strategy.

In scopeOut of scope
Introduction to cloud computing; cloud economics; providers and ecosystems; architecture; security and compliance; migration strategies; managing cloud operations; governance; disaster recovery and business continuityDeep technical training and hands-on configuration of cloud tools

Physiotherapy Assistant (ed2go): global course edits

Course-wide principles agreed before any lesson-level work, each with the learning designer's note, the SME's response, the education specialist's note, and an approval.

Global editDecision
Revise all writing to reflect Canadian spelling and grammarApply course-wide
Revise all language related to certificationRetain as the course-wide principle pending the client's decision between two certification options
Revise all language regarding governing bodiesRetain as a principle; resolve lesson by lesson, no global find-and-replace
Fact check all revised language against Canadian sourcesRetain as a process requirement
Review and revise imagery and proper nouns for a Canadian contextApply a Canadian standard to imagery and names

The lesson-level tracker that follows carries one row per page element for each of the 26 lessons, with a revision category, the proposed change, and the alignment to the revised objectives.
